VRNT Engineers (developed by P.V. Nimantha Madhushan & P.V. Ruwantha Madhushan) provides a construction invoicing and job management mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use the app. By using VRNT Engineers, you agree to the practices outlined here.

📋 1. Information We Collect

To deliver the invoicing & management features, VRNT Engineers collects the following categories:

🔐 Account Data Email address & password (used for secure authentication).
👤 Profile Data Optional profile picture — stored securely and visible only to you.
🏦 Business/Financial Data Bank account details (Account Name, Account Number, Bank Name, Branch) inserted by users to embed into invoices.
📇 Client & Job Data Client names, phone numbers, job categories (Roof, Custom, etc.), cost calculations, calendar history and invoice details.

📲 2. Device Permissions Required

  • Storage / Files — to save generated PDF invoices directly on your device.
  • Camera / Photo Library — to allow you to upload a profile picture (optional).
  • Internet / Network — required to synchronize data with our backend (Supabase) and enable authentication.

You can change permission settings anytime from your mobile device settings, but some features may be limited.

⚙️ 3. Third-Party Services & Technologies

We rely on trusted services to provide robust functionality:

Supabase — user authentication (secure email/password login) & real-time database. All synced data is stored on encrypted Supabase cloud servers. Hive (local storage) — lightweight NoSQL database to store price settings, custom categories, and bank details locally for offline access and faster performance. Share Plus — enables exporting and sharing PDF invoices to external apps (WhatsApp, email, etc.) directly from the app. PDF generation (local) — all invoices are generated natively on-device; no invoice data is sent to external print servers.

🔹 No advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers are integrated. Your data remains under your control and our limited backend scope.

🎯 4. How We Use Your Information

  • ✓ Provide, maintain, and improve invoicing functionality (creating, editing, and sharing invoices).
  • ✓ Enable secure login and cross-device data access (sync via Supabase).
  • ✓ Generate professional PDF invoices with your company and client details.
  • ✓ Store preferences locally (categories, price defaults) for offline productivity.
  • ✓ We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data, client data, or financial information to third parties.

🔒 5. Data Storage & Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • Authentication: Supabase handles authentication with hashed passwords and secure session tokens.
  • Encryption in transit: All communication between the app and Supabase uses TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Local storage: Hive boxes are stored inside the app's private directory; inaccessible to other apps.
  • Data retention: You can edit or delete any client/job data at any time. If you delete your account, all related data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.
  • While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your device updated.

👥 6. User Rights & Control

As a user of VRNT Engineers, you have the following rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of your stored data (email us).
  • Correction: Edit your profile, bank details, or client info inside the app at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete individual invoices, clients, or your entire account via settings.
  • Opt-out of optional data: Profile picture is strictly optional; you can remove it any time.
  • To exercise rights, contact 01nimantha@gmail.com or 01ruwantha@gmail.com.

🧸 7. Children’s Privacy

VRNT Engineers is not directed toward individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. For significant updates, we may notify you via in-app notice or email (if available). Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Limited data access is provided only to core infrastructure (Supabase) under strict data processing agreements. Your clients’ information (names, phone numbers, job details) is only used to generate invoices that you initiate. No analytics or tracking frameworks are present. The app fully operates under applicable privacy laws including GDPR (for European users) and CCPA principles — you have the right to request data erasure.
